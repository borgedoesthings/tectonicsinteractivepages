Blog/Updates

5/13

Hey all! I decided to put together a small page here entirely for updates on current devving that will be updated either weekly or monthly depending on whats going on. So every Friday(or the first Friday of the month) we will let you in a bit on the current status of the projects were working on and give some dev insights on upcoming features or mechanics we're planning on implementing. We at the team thought it would be fun to write these down and look back on our journey with our projects but also with the studio as a whole. As for this very first update I dont have too much to bring you! Just know that me, George, TC and Tsheezus have been hard at work writing music for Endgame++ so a lot of the pushing to the repo is stopped especially since a lot of the team was busy finishing up with their finals for college. We're aware that the first deadline for Endgame++ was unfortunately missed and our sighst were set a bit too high, but we're all learning how to manage this with our other obligations. Right now this whole project is a passion project that we all want to succeed but we may have bit off a bit more than we can chew for our first deadline. This falls on me the most since I'm the one managing this team of amazing people and this is my first time working on/leading a project of this scale. Other than that we will update the Endgame++ page later and any announcements with stuff to chare will be put there first. Other than that I hope you all have an amazing Wednesday, and we will see all of you next Friday with more information! - borge
